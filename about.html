Designer & environment artist telling stories through light and place.

I'm Pranitha Andra, a designer and visual development artist based in San Jose, California. My work lives where storytelling meets space, designing the props, characters, and environments that give a scene its mood before a single frame is animated.

Across projects like Dream?, Heavy Bloom, and Corner Stop, I develop the look of a world from first thumbnail to final color key: layout, lighting, palette, and the small details that make a place feel lived-in.

Alongside visual development I work in graphic design and branding, and I keep my eye sharp with reference studies, film studies, and photobashed environments. I'm currently available for freelance and full-time opportunities.
